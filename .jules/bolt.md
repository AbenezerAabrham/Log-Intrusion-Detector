## 2026-08-05 - Optimizing Log Analyzer Core Loop
**Learning:** High-throughput client-side log parsing suffers from high Garbage Collection overhead and redundant operations when splitting datasets and executing dynamic regex maps sequentially. In particular, utilizing `Object.entries(PATTERNS).forEach` and allocating new sub-arrays for filtered line items on every single log line dramatically reduces performance.
**Action:** Hoist the parsed rule arrays and constant regexes outside of the loop structure, avoid intermediate line-filtering arrays by keeping raw split lines intact and skipping empty ones inline, cache repeat lookups such as `ipStats[ip]`, and use high-performance standard index-based `for` loops with unary numeric conversions instead of utility wrappers or `parseInt`.
